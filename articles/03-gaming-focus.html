Chase Arenella: Why Gaming Sharpens Focus & Strategy

Chase Arenella · Video Game Enthusiast · Project Management

I’m Chase Arenella, and I’ve always seen video games as more than entertainment. Good games are systems: they reward observation, planning, adaptation, and teamwork. Those habits transfer directly to high-stakes work and fast-moving teams.

1) Systems thinking

Games teach you to understand the rules of the system and optimize within constraints. That’s the same mental muscle used in project planning: dependencies, tradeoffs, and sequencing.

2) Decision-making under pressure

Whether it’s a timed objective or a competitive match, games train you to make good-enough decisions quickly and then iterate. That reduces paralysis and increases momentum in real work.

3) Team communication

Multiplayer games are a crash course in coordination: clear callouts, role coverage, and rapid pivots. Those are the same behaviors that make delivery teams resilient.

Closing

For me, gaming reinforces a practical mindset: learn the system, communicate clearly, adapt fast, and keep moving forward. That’s part of how I approach work and life as Chase Arenella.
